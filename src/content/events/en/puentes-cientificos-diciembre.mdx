# Scientific Bridges: Final Session & Yearly Closing 2026

**CBS-UNMSM** celebrates the conclusion of the academic year with the **Grand Final Session of the Scientific Bridges Series**, bringing together guest keynote speakers, faculty advisors, partner institutions, and community members.

This celebratory session presents a complete retrospective of 2026 achievements, including the International Symposium, BioDesign, BioBoost, and environmental synthetic biology projects like YakuPbubbles.

### Program Overview
- Closing keynote speech by an esteemed international scientist.
- Annual retrospective and presentation of the CBS 2026 Scientific Report.
- Recognition awards for organizers, speakers, and student researchers.
