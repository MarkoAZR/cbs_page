# Synbio Bootcamp & Hackathon 2026

The **CBS-UNMSM** community kicked off 2026 by actively competing in the **Synbio Bootcamp & Hackathon**.

Our team won **First Place** and the **Gold Medal** with **Yaku Pbubbles**, a bacterial chassis designed for efficient absorption and detection of heavy metals in water.
