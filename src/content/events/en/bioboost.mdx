# BioBoost 2026: Elevating Scientific & Professional Profiles

**BioBoost 2026** is an intensive development initiative organized by **CBS-UNMSM** to empower the next generation of bioscientists with high-impact skills.

The program bridges wet lab precision, computational biology capabilities, and key interpersonal leadership qualities required in academia and biotech industries.

### Core Pillars
- **Digital Tools & Bioinformatics:** Modern workflows for genomic analysis and in silico plasmid design.
- **Laboratory Rigor & Reproducibility:** Standard operating procedures, experimental design, and data integrity.
- **Leadership & Communication:** Pitching science effectively, team dynamics, and competitive applications for global research opportunities.
