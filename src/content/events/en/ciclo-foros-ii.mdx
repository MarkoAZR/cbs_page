# SynBio Forum Cycle: 2nd Edition

We close the academic year with the **Second Edition of the SynBio Forum Cycle**.

Throughout November and December, weekly sessions will be held (similar to a *Journal Club* or specialized seminar format) where community members and guests will discuss recently published articles and new technologies.

### 2nd Edition Themes (Tentative)
- Deep Learning applications in protein and peptide modeling.
- Progress in the Latin American iGEM.
- Review of biotechnological legislation.
- Year-end closing and general presentation of CBS 2026 results.

The forums are characterized by being horizontal spaces, where opinion and critical thinking matter as much as technical background.
