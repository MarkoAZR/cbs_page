# 1st «Recovering Water Value» Symposium — UNALM

**CBS-UNMSM** takes part in the **1st "Recovering Water Value" Symposium**, held at the Universidad Nacional Agraria La Molina (UNALM) campus in Lima, Peru.

Our community will lead an intensive training workshop and showcase our leading environmental synthetic biology initiative.

### Program Highlights
- **In Silico Biosensor Design Workshop:** Computational modeling of genetic circuits engineered to detect heavy metals in aquatic ecosystems.
- **YakuPbubbles Presentation:** Demonstration of our engineered microbial chassis for water bioremediation and pollutant removal.
- **Inter-university Scientific Exchange:** Building research bridges between UNMSM and UNALM.
