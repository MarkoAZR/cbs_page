# BioDesign Tour: Science & Tech for High Schools

As part of the **BioDesign 2026 Bootcamp & Hackathon** initiative, the CBS-UNMSM organizing committee successfully completed the **BioDesign Tour**, an in-person scientific outreach program visiting secondary schools throughout Metropolitan Lima.

This initiative connected modern biotechnology and genetic engineering with high school students, empowering them to tackle environmental and health challenges.

### Scientific and Educational Focus of the Tour
In every school visit, the **CBS-UNMSM** and **NEA** team prioritized demystifying key molecular biology principles through hands-on demos:
- **DNA Structure & Code:** Understanding biological information storage in the double helix and the molecular central dogma.
- **How Does a Gene Work?:** Analyzing mechanisms of transcription (DNA to messenger RNA) and translation into functional proteins.
- **The Functional Gene as a Transcriptional Unit (TU):** Hands-on approach where a gene is engineered and assembled using standardized biological parts: **Promoter** (start signal and transcription strength), **RBS** (ribosome binding site), **CDS / Coding Sequence** (protein instructions), and **Terminator** (stop signal and stability).

### Participating Educational Institutions
- **IE 1263 Puruchuco (Ate):** Experimental session on DNA double helix architecture and modular TU design.
- **IE 1264 Juan Andrés Vivanco Amorín (Ate):** Gene expression demonstration, biosafety principles, and bio-circuit logic.
- **IE 7035 Leoncio Prado (San Juan de Miraflores):** Biotechnology discovery, promoter/terminator regulation, and direct STEM vocational guidance.

### Impact & Connection with BioDesign 2026
- **Preferential enrollment:** Students from visited schools receive priority access to the BioDesign 2026 Bootcamp & Hackathon.
- **Direct university mentorship:** Vocational dialogue with San Marcos university researchers and synthetic biology leaders.
