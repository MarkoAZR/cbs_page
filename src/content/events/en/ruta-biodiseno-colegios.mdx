# BioDesign Tour: Science & Tech for High Schools

As part of the **BioDesign 2026 Bootcamp & Hackathon** initiative, the CBS-UNMSM organizing committee leads the **BioDesign Tour**, an on-site outreach program visiting secondary schools throughout Metropolitan Lima.

This initiative connects modern biotechnology and genetic engineering with high school students, empowering them to tackle environmental and health challenges.

### Highlights
- **Hands-on demo workshops:** Interactive in-situ experiments using safe biological modeling kits.
- **Preferential enrollment:** Students from visited schools receive priority access to the BioDesign 2026 Bootcamp & Hackathon.
- **STEM mentorship:** Direct dialogue with UNMSM university researchers and synthetic biology leaders.
