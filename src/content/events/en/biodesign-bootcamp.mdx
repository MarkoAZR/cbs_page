# BioDesign Project 2026 — High School Bootcamp & Hackathon

Co-organized by **CBS-UNMSM** and **NEA (Neuronas en Acción)**, **BioDesign 2026** is the country's most radical youth bioengineering initiative.

The journey kicks off with the **In-Person Opening Ceremony** (Saturday, September 26, 2:00 PM at the Faculty of Mathematical Sciences Auditorium, UNMSM — entry strictly via Gate 2), leading into an intensive **Virtual Bootcamp** and the **Core Hackathon Sprint** (October 9–23), concluding with in-person project defense and awards ceremony in Lima.

### Live Opening Ceremony
- **Date & Time:** Saturday, September 26, 2:00 PM (Check-in from 1:30 PM).
- **Venue:** Faculty of Mathematical Sciences Auditorium — National University of San Marcos (UNMSM).
- **Access:** Pedestrian entry **strictly through Gate 2** (Av. Germán Amézaga).
- **Requirement:** Original **physical ID** is mandatory for campus gate control.

### Core October Milestones
- **October 9:** Informative kick-off and official challenge prompts delivery.
- **October 10–11:** 48-hour collaborative genetic circuit modeling sprint on Benchling.
- **October 12–22:** Asynchronous partial evaluation by the jury panel under the official rubric.
- **October 23:** Grand Final Pitch, oral defense, and Top 3 teams awards ceremony.
