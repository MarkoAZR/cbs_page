# BioDesign Project 2026 — High School Bootcamp & Hackathon

Co-organized by **CBS-UNMSM** and **NEA (Neuronas en Acción)**, **BioDesign 2026** is the country's most radical youth bioengineering initiative.

> **Top 10 Announced!** After the jury deliberation on the 17 submitted projects, we now know the 10 teams advancing to the live Grand Final on **Friday, October 30 at 2:00 PM** at UNMSM. The list is ordered by team number and **does not indicate merit**.

### Core October Milestones
- **Friday, October 9 (6:00 PM):** Official kick-off, track challenge prompts release, and submission templates.
- **Saturday, Oct 10 & Sunday, Oct 11:** 48-hour collaborative genetic circuit modeling sprint on Benchling with live mentorship and Discord support.
- **Sunday, October 11 (11:59 PM):** Formal deadline for project submission (Summary Sheet, Benchling link, and pitch video).
- **October 12–25:** Technical evaluation by a pool of 6 specialist judges (80%) on the 17 submitted projects.
- **October 25:** ✅ Official announcement of the Top 10 national finalist teams.
- **Friday, October 30 (2:00 – 5:00 PM):** Live Grand Final Pitch at the UNMSM auditorium (1 representative per team, 4-min pitch + 3-min Q&A, virtual for regional delegations) before the expert evaluation panel (20%), deliberation, and official awards. Pedestrian entrance through Gate No. 2; up to 2 companions per student.
