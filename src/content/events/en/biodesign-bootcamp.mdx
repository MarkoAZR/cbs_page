# BioDesign Project 2026 — High School Bootcamp & Hackathon

Co-organized by **CBS-UNMSM** and **NEA (Neuronas en Acción)**, with official institutional endorsement from **SENAJU**, **BioDesign 2026** is the country's most radical youth bioengineering initiative.

The journey combines an intensive **Virtual Bootcamp** with the **Core Hackathon Sprint** (October 9–23), concluding with in-person project defense and awards ceremony in Lima.

### Core October Milestones
- **October 9:** Informative kick-off and official challenge prompts delivery.
- **October 10–11:** 48-hour collaborative genetic circuit modeling sprint on Benchling.
- **October 12–22:** Asynchronous partial evaluation by the jury panel under the official rubric.
- **October 23:** Grand Final Pitch, oral defense, and Top 3 teams awards ceremony.
