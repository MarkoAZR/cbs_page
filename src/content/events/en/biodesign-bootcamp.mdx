# BioDesign Project 2026 — High School Bootcamp & Hackathon

Co-organized by **CBS-UNMSM** and **NEA (Neuronas en Acción)**, **BioDesign 2026** is the country's most radical youth bioengineering initiative.

> **Extended Call!** Team and individual registrations have been extended until **Friday, September 25, 2026**.

### Official Opening Ceremony (100% Virtual)
- **Date & Time:** Monday, September 28, 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM (Room opens at 4:50 PM).
- **Format:** Live virtual room (Online connection).
- **Institutional Statement:** We extend our sincere apologies for any logistical disruption this rescheduling to a virtual format may cause to your calendars. This adjustment ensures the complete integration of all high school delegations registered from provinces across Peru.
- **Secure Access:** For student safety and privacy, private room credentials are sent directly to the registered email addresses of tutors and participants.
- **Continuity:** At 6:00 PM, the ceremony transitions seamlessly into **Bootcamp Session 1: DNA & the Manual of Life**.

### Core October Milestones
- **October 9:** Informative kick-off and official challenge prompts delivery.
- **October 10–11:** 48-hour collaborative genetic circuit modeling sprint on Benchling.
- **October 12–22:** Asynchronous partial evaluation by the jury panel under the official rubric.
- **October 23:** Grand Final Pitch, oral defense, and Top 3 teams awards ceremony in Lima.
