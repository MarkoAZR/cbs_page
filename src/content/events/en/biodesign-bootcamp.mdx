# BioDesign Project 2026 — High School Bootcamp & Hackathon

Co-organized by **CBS-UNMSM** and **NEA (Neuronas en Acción)**, **BioDesign 2026** is the country's most radical youth bioengineering initiative.

> **Active Hackathon Sprint!** School delegations begin the 48-hour intensive in silico co-design sprint on Benchling this **Friday, October 9 at 6:00 PM**.

### Core October Milestones
- **Friday, October 9 (6:00 PM):** Official kick-off, track challenge prompts release, and submission templates.
- **Saturday, Oct 10 & Sunday, Oct 11:** 48-hour collaborative genetic circuit modeling sprint on Benchling with live mentorship and Discord support.
- **Sunday, October 11 (6:59 PM):** Formal deadline for project submission (Summary PDF, Benchling link, and Pitch Deck with 3-minute video).
- **October 12–19:** Asynchronous technical evaluation by a pool of 6 specialist judges (80%).
- **Tuesday, October 20:** Official announcement of the Top 15 national finalist teams.
- **Friday, October 23:** Grand Final Pitch in Lima before the expert evaluation panel (20%), deliberation, and awards ceremony.
