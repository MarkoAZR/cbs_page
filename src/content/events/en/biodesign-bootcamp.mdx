# BioDesign 2026 Proceedings — High School Bootcamp & Hackathon

Co-organized by **CBS-UNMSM** and **NEA (Neuronas en Acción)**, **BioDesign 2026** successfully concluded as Peru's first national immersion program in synthetic biology and *in silico* genetic engineering for secondary school students.

> **Audited Impact:** The 2026 edition brought together **150+ registered students** across **9 regions of Peru** (Lima & Callao, Tacna, Cusco, Piura, Áncash, Junín, Huancavelica, Ayacucho, and La Libertad), forming **27 teams**, **17 evaluated complete projects**, and **10 finalist delegations** that defended their biocircuits live on **October 30, 2026** at the UNMSM Rosa Alarco Larrabure Auditorium.

### Completed Program Milestones
- **August – September 2026:** In-Person School Tour in Lima (Colegio Claretiano, Colegio Cristo Rey, and Colegio Parroquial San Ricardo).
- **September 28 – October 7:** 5-session Virtual Bootcamp with ~66% sustained attendance.
- **October 9 – 11:** 48-hour *in silico* genetic modeling Hackathon Sprint in Benchling.
- **October 30, 2026:** In-Person Grand Final, live oral defense before the evaluating jury (Dr. Daniel Guerra Giráldez, Mario Taira Custodio, and Marko A. Zapata Roca), and official award ceremony at UNMSM.
