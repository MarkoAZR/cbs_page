# SynBio Lab Cycle

From **June to August**, we will begin our lab cycle focused on the design, behavior, and deployment of **biosensors** using synthetic biology.

A bacterial biosensor detects specific components in a medium by triggering a biological circuit in response to an external stimulus (e.g., the presence of metals like Lead or Cadmium).

### Cycle Objectives
- Understand the fundamentals of conditional transcription and transcription factors.
- Study reference papers on GFP and bioluminescence systems in model organisms.
- Integrate this knowledge into refining the computational system behind **AURA-Bio**.

This multi-month cycle will provide participants with direct tools that impact the Sustainable Development Goals.
